# Users & Personas

Drawn from Kyle's real following (anonymized). Every account is both buyer and seller; personas describe the *intent* they show up with.

## 1. The Grail Hunter
**JTBD:** "Get me the '86 Fleer Jordan (or cards from that set) without getting gouged."
Always looking; constantly upcharged because demand outstrips visible supply. On Exprifi they name their price and actually get the card. High willingness to pay the 5% convenience fee — certainty is the product.

## 2. The Dead-Inventory Seller
**JTBD:** "Move the stuff nobody around me seems to want."
Sitting on stagnant singles/lots. It feels like nobody wants this one thing — then a funded buyer for exactly it pops up on the board. Global audience instead of local network; may even sell at a premium. Zero seller fees is the recruiting hook.

## 3. The Efficient Price-Locker
**JTBD:** "Lock a fair price fast, on either side of the table."
Values the structured Accept/Counter/Decline flow over haggling in DMs. Buyer or seller by the day. Cares about speed, comps, and no-nonsense settlement.

## 4. The High-End Singles Buyer
**JTBD:** "Source graded, high-value cards on my terms, discreetly."
The up-market climb target (not the launch persona). Where finder's-fee revenue lives. Needs the trust story (photos, escrow, reputation) to be solid before they arrive.

## 5. The Whatnot Streamer
**JTBD:** "Keep my stream stocked with bulk I can mark up."
Buys large-volume inventory across all sports; sells live. Exprifi is their supply chain — post a filter need ("5k-ct box, 2019–21 basketball, rookies in"), get racing offers, restock weekly. Future: Disney + TCG when Exprifi expands. This persona IS the both-sides seed user.

## 0. Kyle himself (founder-user)
VoloksVault flips cards ~2hrs/day — every feature gets dogfooded against a real reseller workflow. If it doesn't fit his packing/listing/sorting rhythm, it won't fit the beachhead's.
