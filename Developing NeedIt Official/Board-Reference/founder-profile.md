# Founder Profile — Kyle (added: the board needs this context; not in the original 12)

## Snapshot
23, first-year full-time teacher (starting fall 2026; summer school now), Connecticut. Runs VoloksVault (card flipping, ~2hrs/day) alongside. Got hooked on cards while recovering on the couch in February. Paycheck-to-paycheck now; ~$60k salary starts with the school year. Exprifi is the dream.

## The Amazon framing (his words)
"Amazon started with books and became the giant it is — that's how I look at Exprifi. I want to top eBay." End goal: build it Barstool-style — live the CEO life, pour everything in, sell to a larger company (plausibly eBay) when it's time. No quick flip. Would take outside money. Personal win condition: pay off college for all his boys from home.

## Strengths / weaknesses (self-assessed — advisors calibrate here)
- **Trust him on:** vision, desire, ideas (hundreds/day in his phone), community instinct, the hobby itself.
- **Push him on:** execution (only ~1% of ideas ship), numbers/finance (explicitly wants expertise here), saying no (selflessness gets exploited; people-pleasing eats Exprifi time), avoidance spirals (Instagram holes driven by fear/anxiety about Exprifi's potential), customer-service dread.
- **Decision he's pre-worrying:** when to leave teaching. Rule he's set: Exprifi must grow without hurting the teaching career; he only leaves if it explodes. He loves the school system.

## Weekly schedule (school year)
- **M–F 8:00–4:00:** out of house for school (teachers get many days off — usable for deep work)
- **After school:** gym (wants it), then VoloksVault tasks (~2hrs: packing, listing, sleeving, sorting — mostly filmed), then Exprifi *if energy remains* — which is the problem
- **Tue 8pm:** Whatnot stream (weekly; considering experimenting with 5am streams given evening competition)
- **Weekends:** summer = constant travel (should ease in fall); occasional going out; family calls (says yes even when it costs a planned Exprifi day)
- **Known failure modes:** working from bed → naps; no fixed Exprifi block; content editing sinkhole (~1hr per 10–30s video, 3 months of backlog)

## What the board should engineer around
1. A **fixed, protected Exprifi block** (not in bed, not "after everything else") — the single highest-leverage schedule change.
2. **Execution scaffolding:** small shippable commitments per week, not idea capture.
3. **Content pipeline relief:** the 3-month footage backlog is a prerequisite he's set for audience growth — reduce per-video cost or batch it.
4. **CS-load fear:** address structurally (see trust-and-safety.md) so it stops blocking launch courage.
