# Glossary

Internal terms — use these consistently in all prompts, docs, and UI copy.

## Platform terms
- **Exprifi** — public brand ("Express Fulfillment": *expr-* = express what you want, *-ifi* = fulfill it). Never say "needit" publicly.
- **needit** — internal codename only (repo, Supabase project, folders).
- **Need** — a buyer's posted demand: the listing. Single or bulk/filter. Has budget, condition, expiry.
- **Want** — a private, unpublished need (wishlist). Publishing = "putting the call out."
- **The Board** — the public feed of open needs (home page).
- **Offer** — a seller's structured response to a need: price + condition + photo + note. Private to the buyer.
- **Counter** — a structured price counter-offer (Accept/Counter/Decline; 10-round cap).
- **Match** — buyer accepts an offer; siblings auto-decline, identities reveal, deal record created.
- **Deal** — a matched transaction (Bought/Sold in completed-deals).
- **Lane 2** — open request board (built; the MVP).
- **Lane 1** — instant price-match engine against seller inventory (M2; the moat). Includes the **race** (sellers compete to accept first) and the load-bearing **final confirm**.
- **Filter request** — a bulk need defined by criteria, not a specific card ("any PSA 8+ vintage Yankees under $200").
- **Leak / leak defense** — parties meeting on-platform, transacting off. Countered by structured offers, masked identity, escrow.
- **Concierge seeding** — Kyle manually guaranteeing early liquidity ("post it, I'll make sellers show up").
- **Convenience fee** — the 5% buyer-side fee (Model B); **finder's fee** — the 5% high-end seller-side fee (Model A). See fees-and-monetization.md.
- **VoloksVault** — Kyle's card business + audience (IG/TikTok/Whatnot). The seed community.
- **Top5** — unrelated future idea of Kyle's; parking-lot it.

## Hobby terms
- **Bulk** — large low-value lots (the "treasure hunt" segment); opposite of singles.
- **Singles** — individual cards; **high-end singles** = the up-market revenue segment.
- **Breaker / break** — someone who opens sealed product live and sells shares/spots; both buyer and seller.
- **Wax** — sealed, unopened product (boxes/packs).
- **Slab** — a card professionally graded and sealed in a plastic case.
- **PSA / SGC / Beckett (BGS)** — the three major grading companies; "PSA 9" = condition shorthand.
- **Raw** — ungraded card.
- **Sleeved** — card in a penny sleeve. **Toploaded** — sleeved card inside a rigid toploader.
- **Comp** — recent comparable sale price; the ground truth for "listed above comp."
- **WTB** — "want to buy," the Facebook-group post format Exprifi structures into needs.
