# Brand

## Name rationale
**Exprifi = "Express Fulfillment."** *Expr-* = you express/put out what you want; *-ifi* = the physical act of fulfilling the order (the seller's side). Born from the hunt for an available .com — exprifi.com is owned (Namecheap; still parked, app lives at need-it.vercel.app until pointed). "needit" is the internal codename only (repo, Supabase, folders) — never public.

## Taglines (Kyle's working set)
- **The demand exchange — post what you want, sellers come to you.** (lead candidate)
- Post a need. Watch sellers race to fill it.
- Browse open needs. Be first to strike the deal.
- The marketplace that hunts for you.
- Open demand.
- Demand becomes the listing. (thesis line, more internal/investor-facing)

## Voice & tone
Collector energy with a trustworthy spine. The venue must look legit — trust is a conversion input: sellers racing to win a buyer need to believe the platform is real. Hype where it drives the race ("sellers racing," countdowns, "put the call out"); clean and precise everywhere money or identity is involved. Kyle's authentic register is treasure-hunt enthusiasm — the lingo of bulk, breaks, and flips (see glossary.md).

## Visual identity — LOCKED (direction 3a)
Full spec: `exprifi-brand-system.md` in the working folder. Summary:
- **Accent green `#00A968`** as the single strong accent
- **Dark live-board aesthetic** — the board feels like a live exchange, not a static catalog
- **Instrument Sans** typography
- Card-photo-forward, StockX/Whatnot-adjacent modernity; framing is **intent, not account type** ("Find cards / Sell cards" — every account is both)

## Personality
The sharp friend at the card show who knows a guy for everything: hustler's speed, collector's obsession, straight shooter on price. Never corporate, never sloppy about trust.
