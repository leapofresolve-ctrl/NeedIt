# Positioning

## Who we win first
1. **Bulk resellers & breakers** (beachhead) — buyer AND seller at once; one recruited user seeds both sides. High inventory, high listing friction; a feed of purchase intent beats another listing slot.
2. **Treasure-hunt / low-end buyers** — the crowd Kyle has a following in (VoloksVault). Their deals literally cannot happen on eBay search.
3. **High-value singles buyers/sellers** (up-market climb) — where finder's-fee revenue lives. Entered only after bulk liquidity is proven.

## Target buyer
Knows exactly what they want (a card, a set, or a filter like "any PSA 8+ vintage Yankees under $200"), tired of overpaying against thin supply and grinding saved searches. Wants price certainty and speed.

## Target seller
Sitting on inventory — dead singles, bulk boxes, post-break leftovers. Wants demand to come to them, hates fees, values speed-to-cash.

## Competitive comparison
| Platform | Model | Their weakness we exploit |
|---|---|---|
| **eBay** | Supply-side listings + auctions | Demand invisible; overpriced thin-stock listings; ~13% seller fees; search grind. **The real enemy — and the hoped-for acquirer.** |
| **COMC / MySlabs** | Card-specific listing sites | Same supply-side model, smaller reach; no bulk/filter demand capture. |
| **Whatnot** | Live-stream selling | **Partner, not enemy:** Exprifi is the perfect bulk-inventory feeder for Whatnot streamers — they source lots on Exprifi, mark up on stream. |
| **Facebook groups** | Ad-hoc want-to-buy posts | Our closest behavioral competitor — chaotic, scam-prone, unsearchable. **Frenemy: primary launch/hype channel.** |
| **Fanatics ecosystem** | Vertically integrated hobby giant | Strong network; likely to see us as a threat rather than partner. Monitor. |
| **Amazon / Mercari / Etsy / Poshmark** | Generalist supply-side | No hobby trust, no demand-side mechanic, no card-collector culture. Not where these deals happen. |
| **StockX** | Bid/ask exchange | Closest mechanical cousin (bids ≈ demand), but only for catalog-standard items — useless for bulk/filter requests, which is exactly our beachhead. |

## Positioning statement
For collectors and resellers who know what they want, Exprifi is the demand exchange where you post the need and sellers race to fill it — instead of searching supply someone else decided to list.

## Sequencing rule (locked)
Win bulk/treasure-hunt first (nobody serves it, Kyle owns the audience), then climb to high-end singles. Never lead with fees/trust/AI in messaging — lead with the demand-side model.
