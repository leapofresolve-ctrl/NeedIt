# Go-to-Market

## Chicken-and-egg answer: seed BOTH sides with one persona
The beachhead user — bulk resellers/breakers — is buyer and seller at once. Recruiting one seeds both sides. This is the structural cheat that makes cold start survivable.

## Launch plan (Aug 8, 2026)
1. **Seed from Kyle's own audience** (VoloksVault: IG + TikTok, ~900 Whatnot followers, weekly Tuesday 8pm streams).
2. **Facebook groups as the hype channel** — the frenemy: where these deals happen today, and where launch gets announced. Convert WTB-post culture into needs on the board.
3. **Concierge seeding:** "post what you need, I'll make sure sellers show up." Kyle personally guarantees early liquidity.
4. **Content engine:** 3 months of unedited footage backlogged; edit + post ASAP to rebuild momentum before launch. Higgsfield (prepaid annual) for ad creative; ~$200/mo ad budget after creative exists.

## Growth loops
- **Seller race loop:** need posted → offer-count badge visible → sellers compete → buyer matched fast → posts next need.
- **Both-sides loop:** seller wins a deal → has needs of their own (restock) → posts them.
- **Whatnot feeder loop:** streamers source bulk lots on Exprifi → mark up on stream → return whenever they need product. Positioning: *we are Whatnot's supply chain, not its competitor.*
- **Demand-alert loop (planned):** sellers subscribe to what buyers want — the inverted saved search brings them back daily.

## ⚠️ Stealth vs. liquidity — open tension (board item)
Kyle's worry: launching loudly to 1,000+ people tips off eBay ("their extensive team redevelops their app and we lose everything") — he hasn't bought into the M1 liquidity test. Counterpoints the board should weigh: (a) incumbents rarely cannibalize their listing-fee model to copy a zero-revenue startup; eBay already retired similar mechanics; (b) the defensible asset is the demand graph + community, which only accrues by launching; (c) a marketplace kept quiet dies of emptiness, the far likelier failure mode. Resolution needed before Aug 8: launch loud vs. staged rollout (e.g., invite waves through trusted FB groups).

## Launch markets
US-only, all sports, vintage → current. No geo strategy needed pre-scale (shipping is parties' own concern pre-escrow).

## Up-market climb (after bulk liquidity)
High-value singles buyers/sellers — arrive once the board demonstrably fills needs; finder's-fee revenue follows them.
