# Metrics

## North star (M1 — until liquidity is proven)
1. **% of needs receiving ≥1 offer** — provisional target: **60%+**
2. **Time-to-first-offer** — provisional target: **< 24 hours**

These two ARE the business right now. Nothing else matters until they pass. Provisional supporting targets for launch (Sat Sep 26, 2026): **50–100 seeded users** (weighted toward breakers/resellers), **majority of matched deals actually completed** (spot-check via concierge follow-up).

*Note: targets are Claude-proposed, not Kyle-committed — Kyle hasn't set hard numbers and is skeptical of the liquidity-test framing itself (see gtm.md stealth-vs-liquidity tension). Board should ratify or revise these numbers explicitly.*

## Marketplace health (post-M1)
- **GMV** (matched deal value; "settled GMV" once Stripe rail is used)
- **Take rate** — currently 0%; target model unresolved (see fees-and-monetization.md)
- **Active buyers / active sellers** (posted a need / sent an offer in 30d) and the ratio between them
- **Repeat rate:** % of matched buyers who post another need within 30d; % of sellers who offer again
- **Offers per need** (depth of the race) and **counters per negotiation**
- **Match rate:** % of needs that end in an accepted offer before expiry

## Trust metrics
- Deal completion rate (matched → actually transacted)
- Dispute/complaint rate (also a proxy for Kyle's CS-load worry)
- % offers with photos; seller response rate
- Leakage proxy: repeat counterparty pairs who stop matching on-platform

## "Top of the line" definition
Kyle's own framing is qualitative ("top eBay"); the quantitative ladder: M1 liquidity targets → first $100k cumulative GMV → revenue on (M4) → the demand graph large enough that sellers check Exprifi daily like eBay sellers check their saved searches. Kyle explicitly wants outside expertise on the numbers side — a finance/marketplace advisor seat on this board should own this file.
