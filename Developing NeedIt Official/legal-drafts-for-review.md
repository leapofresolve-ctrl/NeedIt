## 5. Posting needs and making offers

When you post a need you are making a **good-faith statement of intent to buy** at or below your stated budget. When you send an offer you are making a **binding offer to sell** the item described, at the price stated, in the condition stated, and you represent that you own it or have the right to sell it.

When a buyer accepts an offer, both parties are expected to complete the transaction promptly and in good faith. Repeatedly posting needs you won't honour, or offers you can't fill, is grounds for suspension.

Offers are visible only to the buyer who posted the need and the seller who sent them. Aggregate counts (for example "3 offers") are public.

This is the one we enforce most seriously, and it exists to protect you as much as us.

You agree **not to** solicit or facilitate a transaction off Exprifi that began on Exprifi. Specifically, you may not post, send, or embed email addresses, phone numbers, social media handles, messaging-app identifiers, payment-app identifiers, or URLs intended to move a deal off-platform — in any field, in any image, or in any username.

Why this matters to you: off-platform deals have no record, no dispute path, and no recourse. Every scam in this hobby starts with "just DM me". Members who take deals off Exprifi lose every protection we offer, and we can't help when it goes wrong.

**Enforcement:** first attempt gets a warning; repeat attempts get a suspension; egregious or repeated cases get a permanent ban. We may remove content and restrict accounts at our discretion.

## What's public

Your **username, display name, open public needs, and aggregate deal statistics** are visible to anyone — this is a public marketplace and demand being visible is the product. You can restrict your profile in Settings.

To run the marketplace, notify you about your activity, prevent fraud and off-platform solicitation, provide support, meet legal obligations, and understand aggregate usage. **We do not sell your personal information. We do not serve third-party advertising.**

## Who we share it with

Service providers only, each doing a specific job: **Supabase** (database and authentication), **Vercel** (hosting), **Resend** (email delivery), **Stripe** (payments). We also share where legally required, or to investigate fraud or a threat to someone's safety. If the business is ever sold, member data may transfer — you'd be notified.

## How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account is open. After you close it, we deactivate immediately, keep the record for **[14 days]** so you can change your mind, then anonymise your profile. **Completed transaction records are retained indefinitely** — they are the ledger the people you traded with relied on, and we need them for tax and dispute purposes. Once anonymised they're no longer linked to you.

Access, correct, or download your data · close your account · turn off email notifications (individually or entirely) · hide your profile · opt out of search-engine indexing. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under GDPR or the CCPA — email **support@exprifi.com** and we'll honour them.

We use encryption in transit, row-level database access controls, and payment handling delegated to Stripe. **No system is perfectly secure**, and we'll tell you promptly if a breach affects you.

Exprifi is not for anyone under **[18]**. We don't knowingly collect data from children; contact us and we'll delete it.

We use cookies that are **strictly necessary** for sign-in and security. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

# 3. Prohibited Items & Counterfeit Policy

**Never allowed on Exprifi:**

1. **Counterfeit, reprinted, altered, or trimmed cards** presented as genuine — including trimmed, recoloured, or re-holdered slabs.
2. **Counterfeit or tampered grading slabs**, or cards represented as graded when they aren't.
3. **Stolen goods**, or items you don't own or have the right to sell.
4. **"Searched" or resealed wax** sold as unsearched, and repacks not clearly labelled as repacks.
5. **Misrepresented bulk** — lot counts, years, sets, or condition that don't match what ships.
6. Anything illegal to sell where either party is located.
7. Items outside our categories (currently sports cards, TCG, and comics).

**Reprints and custom cards are allowed only if clearly and prominently labelled as such** in the title and description. "REPRINT" in small print at the bottom does not count.

**Reporting:** email **support@exprifi.com** with the need or offer and what's wrong. We review every report, typically within 24 hours. Outcomes range from removing content, to reversing a transaction we hold funds for, to permanently banning the account. **Suspected counterfeiting is treated as fraud and results in a permanent ban on the first offence.**

**If you receive a fake:** contact us immediately with photos and your order details. Where we hold the funds, we can reverse the transaction. Where the deal happened off-platform, we can ban the seller but **we cannot recover your money** — which is precisely why §6 of the Terms exists.

# 4. Off-Platform Solicitation Policy

*This expands §6 of the Terms. It is the policy most likely to be tested, so it's written to be quotable at someone.*

**The rule:** deals that start on Exprifi are completed on Exprifi.

**What counts as a violation** — sharing or requesting, anywhere on the platform including images and usernames: email addresses · phone numbers · social media handles · messaging app IDs · payment app IDs (Venmo, PayPal, Zelle, Cash App) · external URLs · or deliberately obfuscated versions of any of these ("kyle at gmail dot com", "my IG is in my bio", spelled-out digits).

**What's fine** — describing an item, discussing price via counters, using the structured questions, and everything support asks you for over email.

**Enforcement ladder:**

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1st | Content blocked, warning issued, logged |
| 2nd | Content removed, 7-day suspension |
| 3rd | Permanent ban |
| Egregious | Immediate permanent ban — e.g. systematic harvesting of members, or an attempted scam |

We detect this automatically at write time and review flags daily. **You can report it too:** support@exprifi.com.

**Why we're strict:** this isn't only about our fees — we charge nothing today and this rule already exists. It's because every serious scam in this hobby begins by moving the conversation somewhere with no record. On Exprifi there's a record of what was offered, agreed, and paid. In a DM there's nothing.
