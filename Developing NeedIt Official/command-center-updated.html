First screen & access — Kyle's asks, answered

AskHow it's built
"First screen should be larger, buttons more accessible"Full-viewport hero. The two intent doors are the buttons — 180px tall click targets with a 56px button inside as the affordance. Above the fold at 375px and 1440px. Real live counts, never faked.
"Shouldn't need an account to see the site"✓ Shipped Jul 29. Public: / · need pages · /u/[username] · /how-it-works · /help · legal. The "Browse the board" door is now an anchor to the live board sitting directly below the hero, not a login redirect. Also unlocks the SEO the plan already wanted — need pages can't be indexed behind a login.
"Must have an account to interact"✓ Shipped Jul 29. Protected: post · offer · counter · accept · alerts · settings · notifications · Stripe. Never redirect to /login — the need page renders, the offer form shows its real structure inert, and a "Sign in to make an offer" bar carries ?next= back to the same need.
"Sign in with username, not just email"One "Email or username" field. Non-email input hits a SECURITY DEFINER resolver that is REVOKEd from anon and authenticated — otherwise it's a username→email enumeration endpoint. Uniform failure message and timing. Rate limited per IP and per identifier ✓ Jul 29.
"Buyer-only vs both, with 'recommended'"Soft split: both choices create the same account row; the difference is one is_seller boolean and reversible in one click from Settings. Recommended = ink border + chip, pre-selected. Not a shiny "MOST POPULAR" ribbon — that's the tacky failure mode for this exact element.
"Little to no options in personal settings"Correct — it was one checkbox. Expands to seven sections: Account · Notifications (a per-event × in-app/email grid) · Buying · Selling · Privacy & safety · Security (MFA, sessions) · Support.

If that ratio is poor with a working board, the problem is the pitch, not the plumbing.
